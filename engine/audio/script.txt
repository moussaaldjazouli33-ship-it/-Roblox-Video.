In this town, everyone has a bunker except me. My name is Trevor. Ten years ago, the zombie apocalypse began, and every family received an underground bunker.
But each bunker was different. Bunker No. 1 was made entirely of glass beneath the ocean. Bunker No. 7 was a luxury mansion with a swimming pool and unlimited food. And Bunker No. 12 had one rule: never make a sound.
But I had no bunker. I slept inside an abandoned gas station, hiding from zombies every night.
Until I noticed something terrifying. Zombies never attacked me. They chased everyone else, but whenever I walked past, they simply stopped and stared.
So one night, I followed them to an abandoned hospital. Beneath it stood a giant metal door marked Bunker 100.
The moment I touched it, the door opened.
Inside, hundreds of monitors showed every bunker in the city. But one screen displayed a strange message: SUBJECT ZERO HAS RETURNED.
Suddenly, every screen turned red. LOCKDOWN INITIATED.
I turned around. Hundreds of zombies were standing behind me. But they weren't attacking. They were bowing.
Then an old recording appeared. A scientist holding a newborn baby.
That baby was me.
Underneath, three words appeared: THE FIRST INFECTED.
That's when I understood. The bunkers weren't built to protect people from zombies. They were built to protect everyone from me.
But suddenly, another message appeared: SUBJECT ZERO ACTIVATED.
A countdown began. 10... 9... 8...
Then every bunker door in the city started opening.
And a voice whispered: Phase Two has begun.
